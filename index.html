About the property

Description property

This attractive four-bedroom bungalow occupies a pleasant site on the edge of Knock, approximately 1.5km from the town centre.

The property is surrounded by mature gardens to both the front and rear, with ample off-street parking. A block-built garage to the rear provides useful additional storage or workshop space.

This owner-occupied home enjoys a convenient location within easy reach of the N17, providing excellent access to surrounding towns and Ireland West Airport Knock. High-speed fibre broadband is also available, making the property well suited to family living and working from home.

Joint Selling Agents: Higgins Auctioneers, Ballyhaunis.
